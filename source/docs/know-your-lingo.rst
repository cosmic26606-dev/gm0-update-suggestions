Know Your Lingo
===============

Many teams may be unfamiliar with the various terms and slang that are often thrown around at competitions and meets. Here we have compiled a short guide of some commonly used terms to get you up to speed.

Competitions
------------

Program Delivery Partner (PDP)
   Director or directors for events and activities in a region.
Scrimmage
   Unofficial competition, usually for team practice before official events. No judging. Does not advance to anything.
League Meet
   Low-stress competition with matches counting towards league rank at a League Tournament. No judging. Sometimes abbreviated as "LM".
Qualifier
   Tournament with 5-6 qualifying matches and double elimination playoff matches. Judging included. Will advance to States/Regional level.
League Tournament
   Tournament with ranked matches and double elimination playoff matches for teams participating in that League. Judging included. Will advance to States/Regional level. Sometimes called a "League Championship", or abbreviated as "LT".
State/Regional Championship
   International championship with ranked matches and double elimination playoff matches. In case of dual-division championships, division winners will compete in a best-of-3 bracket to determine the event winner. Will advance to the World Championship. Sometimes called "States" or "Regionals".
World Championship
   Championship with ranked matches and double elimination playoff matches (including in Finals). Does not advance to anything. Sometimes called "Worlds".
Premier Events
   Official FTC Competitions with ranked matches and double elimination playoff matches. In case of dual-division events, division winners will compete in a best-of-3 bracket to determine the winner. Judging included. Does not advance to anything.
Offseason Events
   Unofficial competitions that (usually) occur after the World Championship. Judging is sometimes included. Does not advance to anything.
Alliance
   Group of two or, in Worlds eliminations, three teams that compete.
Ranking Points (RP)
   Primary basis in rankings at traditional events. For the DECODE season, each team receives 2 RP for winning a qualification match, 1 for tie-ing, and 0 for losing. The RP is then averaged across all matches for the team.
TieBreaker Points (TBP)
   Secondary determinant in team rankings for traditional events, primary determinant for remote events. For the DECODE season, this is split up into two parts; TBP1 and TBP2. TBP1 is used before TBP2, and is
   the alliances/teams autonomous period score. TBP2 is the alliances/teams endgame score. (Whether it is alliances/teams depends on if the team is at traditional events.)
Qualification Match
   Qualifying matches (usually 5) with random alliance partners and
   opponents, that will be played to determine team rankings.
Elimination Match
   2 alliances of 2 teams (or 3 teams at Worlds) each compete in a match to determine the winning alliance. Except for Finals, the winner is sent to the upper bracket, and the loser is sent to the lower bracket. Also known as elim matches, or just elims.
Advancement Points
   Per-team points gained throughout the event for qualification rankings, participation in playoffs, and awards. Teams advance based off how many advancement points they accumulate throughout the event.

Robot Components
----------------

DC Motor
   Open-loop :term:`RS-555 series <RS-550 Series Motor>` 12 volt motors used
   to power mechanisms. Max of 8 for the DECODE season.
Servo
   Closed-loop :term:`servo-motors <Servo>` used for precise movement.
   Controlled by PWM signals. Max of 10 for the DECODE season.
Drivetrain (DT)
   Mechanism responsible for the movement of the robot.
Intake
   Mechanism responsible for picking up game elements from the field by using
   rotational motion.
Claw
   Mechanism responsible for picking up game elements by grasping.
Linear Extension/Slide
   Mechanism responsible for extending parts beyond the starting configuration
   of the robot in a linear fashion.
Arm
   Mechanism responsible for extending the reach of the robot through rotational motion or multi-axis movement.
REV Control Hub
   Integrated Expansion Hub + :term:`Robot Controller` phone.
   It serves many of the same purposes as the Expansion Hub but
   eliminates the need for a :term:`Robot Controller` phone.
   1 Expansion Hub may be used along side 1 Control Hub.
REV Expansion Hub
   Hardware controller for the robot, connected to :term:`Robot Controller`
   phone.
   Controls 4 motor and encoders, 6 servos, and various sensors via digital,
   analog, and I2C ports.
   Maximum of 2 hubs.
Driver Station (DS)
   Android phone connected to the gamepad controller.
   Drivers interact with the DS phone to start/stop robot.
Robot Controller
   Android phone connected to the Expansion Hub.
   Connects to DS phone via WiFi-Direct.
SystemCore
   A new robot controller that (as of late August 2025) is currently being developed by FIRST for FTC and FRC.
Gamepad Controller
   An Xbox or PS4 style controller which the driver uses to control the robot
   during driver-control period. Maximum of 2.
